ChronoScalp installs for the current Windows user into %LOCALAPPDATA%\ChronoScalp.
Administrator rights are not required.

Before you trade, even on a demo account:

- MetaTrader 5 is installed and you are logged into the account you intend to use.
- XAUUSD and EURUSD are visible in Market Watch.
- Turn off sleep and hibernate while the runner should stay connected.

The installer does not enable live trading. CHRONOSCALP_CONFIRM_LIVE stays no
in the new %APPDATA%\ChronoScalp\.env. The logon task starts paper mode only,
and only while this user is signed in. It is not a Windows service.

There is no promise of profit. Read docs\BAZRASI_SADEGHANE_FA.md after install.
